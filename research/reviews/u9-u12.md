# Units 9–12: author research and self-review

Created 2026-11-13. Twenty original lessons, 160 lesson items, eight separate transfer scenarios, and a dialogue and field mission in every lesson. This is AI-assisted original teaching content; no native-speaker or formal proficiency certification is claimed.

## Editorial choices

- Weather and seasonal talk, festival invitations, permission at religious sites, regional travel, problem reporting and relationships are practiced through concrete scenes.
- Grammar precedes sentence construction in each lesson. Sentence cards explicitly teach their pattern; they are not held-out tests.
- Long vowels, doubled consonants, orthographic kana and spoken particle readings are distinct. Explicitly checked じゅっぷん / juppun, いっぱく / ippaku, しゅっぱつ / shuppatsu, こうえん / kōen and the polite past of 楽しい.
- Culture based on original conversation advice is marked `general`, with no invented authority. Factual context cards link the particular public source below.
- Health content teaches reporting symptoms and asking for comprehension support only. It contains no diagnosis, treatment, dosage or emergency-service claim.
- Plans, wishes, invitations, access permissions and confirmed arrangements are kept distinct. Local people are not treated as representatives of all Japan.

## Verified sources and bounded use

- [JNTO weather and geography](https://www.japan.travel/en/plan/weather/): regional and seasonal variation, blossom and autumn-color context; no live forecast or fixed date.
- [JNTO local festivals](https://www.japan.travel/en/guide/get-involved-in-local-festivals/): local context and event-specific participation; no blanket right to join.
- [JNTO shrine and temple traditions](https://www.japan.travel/en/guide/shrine-and-temple-traditions/): distinguish site types and heed photography instructions; do not impose a single ritual everywhere.
- [Japan Tourism Agency ryokan meals](https://www.mlit.go.jp/kankocho/ryokan/list_en-5.html): ask the property about its actual meal arrangements.
- [MAFF SAVOR JAPAN](https://www.maff.go.jp/e/policies/rural_dev/savor/index.html): regional food as local cultural context, not an ingredient guarantee.
- [JNTO customs and etiquette](https://www.japan.travel/en/responsible-travel-guide/japanese-customs-and-etiquette/): consider residential space and local instructions.
- [Tokyo Metropolitan Police lost-property guidance](https://www.keishicho.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/multilingual/english/finding_services/lost_and_found/lost_something.html): identify and contact the relevant operator or place; no guaranteed recovery.
- [Ito Marinetown souvenir guide](https://www.ito-marinetown.co.jp/en/shops_restaurants/pdf/shops_restaurants.pdf): a local primary-source example of food gifts sharing a journey; not a universal gift obligation.
- [Japan Foundation Irodori overview](https://www.irodori.jpf.go.jp/en/about.html) and [Elementary 1 overview](https://www.irodori.jpf.go.jp/en/elementary01/pdf.html): practical communication scope; no textbook passages or exercise copies used.
