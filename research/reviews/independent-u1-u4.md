# Independent content review: units 1–4

Reviewed 2026-11-13 by the agent that authored units 9–12, independently of the units 1–4 author. This is an AI-assisted editorial pass, not native-speaker or professional certification.

## Scope

Read all 20 lesson dialogues, 80 vocabulary/phrase cards, 20 sentence builds, grammar and culture cards, in-lesson scenarios and eight separate assessments. Checked kana against Japanese, Hepburn romanization, particles, register, question intent, translations, choice ambiguity and whether assessment language has been taught.

## Correction applied

- `u1l3` described a **room number**, while its dialogue gives **二階 (nikai), the second floor**. Changed the scene and follow-up scenario to floor information. The Japanese, reading and translation were already correct.

## Findings

- No blocking kana/romanization mismatch or clearly incorrect target Japanese found in this pass. Long vowels and small っ are preserved in the reviewed key forms, including kōhī, kūkō, kippu, roppyaku, happyaku and ōkii.
- Beginner request chunks are useful and appropriately glossed. Short ellipsis at the ticket counter is contextual and does not pretend to be a universal full-sentence template.
- Grammar cards distinguish present/past gratitude without forcing a false exclusive choice; contrastive demonstratives are tied to explicit speaker/listener positions.
- Culture cards use situation-specific wording and defer to actual store/operator instructions. Ingredient questions do not claim that a visual appearance or an answer about meat proves dietary suitability.
- Held-out scenarios use language taught in their own or earlier units. Bilingual choices assess contextual use; they should not be reported as unaided Japanese reading proficiency.
- No formal JLPT, CEFR or native pronunciation claim is warranted from these exercises.
