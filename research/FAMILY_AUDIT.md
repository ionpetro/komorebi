# Existing learning-project audit

Reviewed the local source, README, content contracts, learning research and editorial records for Skepsis (`ancient-greek-explainer`), Guanxi (`mandarin`), Carpe (`latin-panhellenic`) and Gnothi (`ancient-greek-panhellenic`). Also inspected the shared family references in Agora, Oikos and the other course README files.

The user's earlier Guanxi correction explicitly preferred the existing family interface over a new visual direction. Komorebi therefore keeps the established components and navigation, with restrained Japanese artwork and color details.

Skepsis establishes the quality target: guess-first, real recall, transfer, spaced review, forgiving streaks, meaningful feedback and playful short sessions. Guanxi supplies the richer language/culture contract: situated dialogues, speak-aloud missions, separate assessments, voice fallback, script support, source-linked cultural context and local progress portability.

The implementation reuses the mature pure-JavaScript family architecture. It replaces all Mandarin-specific content and practice with Japanese-specific script and reading behavior. It does not copy credentials, environment files, analytics configuration, progress or private records from any other project.

Important failure patterns from earlier projects were treated as acceptance criteria: hidden prerequisites in assessments; ambiguous answers; translation leakage before retrieval; early practice moving review dates; graded listening without a voice; duplicate sentence chunks; and inflated matching accuracy after wrong attempts.
